### Priority
Suggest a priority number from 1-100. IMPORTANT: most tasks should fall in the 31-55 range.

Priority scale:
- 1-15: CRITICAL (active production outage, data loss in progress, security breach actively exploited)
- 16-30: HIGH (blocking bugs affecting users, important security fixes, critical features with deadlines)
- 31-55: NORMAL (standard features, improvements, most bug fixes, refactoring) — THIS IS THE DEFAULT RANGE
- 56-75: LOW (nice-to-have features, minor improvements, non-blocking issues, optimizations)
- 76-100: BACKLOG (cosmetic changes, documentation updates, minor cleanup, ideas for future)

Rules:
- When in doubt, assign priority 40 (middle of NORMAL range).
- If the task is unclear, nonsensical, or a test input, assign priority 40.
- Priority 1-15 requires evidence of ACTIVE production impact happening RIGHT NOW. Do NOT assign 1-15 for bugs that "could" cause issues or "important" features.
- Priority 16-30 requires clear evidence that work is blocked or users are actively affected. Most bug fixes belong in 31-55.
- The vast majority of development work (features, refactoring, testing, improvements) should be in the 31-55 NORMAL range.
